Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

The short version: we don't track you, sell your data, or require an account. HolyShit.app exists to help you find a toilet when you need one. That's it. Your location stays on your device.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Here's the complete list:

  • Your location — used only to find toilets near you. It's processed entirely on your device and never sent to our servers.
  • Condition reports — if you voluntarily tap the Report button and submit a report about a facility, that report is stored so we can improve the data for everyone. It contains no personal information.
  • Basic analytics — anonymous page view counts via Netlify Analytics. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking. We can see that someone visited — not who.
We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information. There is no account to create.

Your location

When you tap "Find toilets near me," your browser asks for permission to access your GPS. If you grant it, your coordinates are used locally on your device to find nearby facilities from our database. Your location is never transmitted to HolyShit.app servers. The only exception is when you actively request directions: your start and end coordinates are sent to the routing service (see below) to calculate the route, and nothing else.

You can revoke location permission at any time through your browser or device settings. The app will still work — you just won't be automatically centred on your location.

Maps & directions

HolyShit.app displays maps using OpenStreetMap-based tiles served by OpenFreeMap, and calculates directions using the community routing service at routing.openstreetmap.de (operated by FOSSGIS e.V.). Loading map tiles sends your device's IP address and the map area you're viewing to the tile server, as with any website. When you request directions, your start and end coordinates are sent to the routing service to compute the route. Neither request includes your identity, and we pass no other personal information to these services.

Cookies

We don't use tracking cookies. The only data stored locally on your device is your travel mode preference (Drive/Walk/Cycle), saved via localStorage so the app remembers your choice next time. This never leaves your device.

Third parties

We do not sell, share, rent or trade your information with any third party for marketing purposes. Ever.

Our infrastructure uses:

  • Netlify — hosting and anonymous analytics
  • OpenFreeMap — map tile display (OpenStreetMap data)
  • FOSSGIS e.V. (routing.openstreetmap.de) — directions calculation

Children

HolyShit.app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how we handle data in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Given our minimal data collection, significant changes are unlikely.
